nook reads publicly visible, logged-out Airbnb pages at personal, single-user scale. Airbnb's Terms prohibit automated access and `robots.txt` disallows search paths; expect breakage and use at your discretion. If Airbnb blocks nook, the correct response is to stop, not to evade. Not legal advice.
