---
title: Checking availability
description: The wedge — a free forward availability calendar with per-day available, min-nights, and price.
---

`nook availability <id>` returns a forward per-day calendar — `available`, `minNights`, and `price` for each date — over `--months 1..12` or an explicit `--start`/`--end` window. This is the piece no other free agent tool gives you.
